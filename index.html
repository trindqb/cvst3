About

I am a researcher passionate about advancing technology at the cutting edge of modern telecommunications and artificial intelligence. My work spans across 5G networks, machine learning, augmented and virtual reality, and developing AI systems that are more transparent and interpretable.

With a focus on both theoretical foundations and practical applications, I strive to bridge the gap between academic research and real-world implementation. My goal is to create technology that not only performs well but also is understandable and trustworthy.
